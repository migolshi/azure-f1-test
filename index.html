Formula 1

Formula 1 is the highest level of international single-seater racing. Teams compete across multiple Grand Prix weekends around the world, combining cutting-edge engineering with elite driver performance.

What Makes F1 Unique?

  • Cars can exceed 350 km/h on long straights.
  • Teams design their own aerodynamics, engines, and strategy.
  • Race weekends include practice sessions, qualifying, and the Grand Prix.

Strategy Matters

Formula 1 is not just about speed. Tire management, pit stop timing, fuel load, and weather conditions all affect race outcomes. A single strategic decision can determine whether a driver finishes on the podium or outside the points.
